In these past few days, I have been on a journey towards learning how to leverage AI.
Using AI does not mean to let it do all the work but to use it as a learning tool by
leveraging it to help me brainstorm gaps I have missed in my way of thinking. This is because as a 
data analyst, I need to find insights within the data that may be overlooked or may be valuable to the overall outlook.
This is important because it helps me learn how to explain my thinking process and what I am doing step by step.

Something I have grown to learn and love is how valuable applying programmatic thinking is in my day to day life.
This involves breaking down a problem to smaller problems, solving each problem one step at a time then combining all 
the solutions. Structure has become a part of my way of thinking now.

Another important aspect was learning the value of flowcharts and how to draw them.
Drawing out the entire workflow worked really well for me to map out the flow of data.
This is because, I was able to understand how valuable different conditions are and how they affect the overall output. 
Setting a hierarchy or an order for the different conditions is really valuable. At times as analysts we come to understand
the relationship between conditions or variables too late which may affect the output or how the output is displayed.

I'm eager to learn more and see how much more this process will help me in the long run.
